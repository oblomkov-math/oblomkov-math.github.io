\section{Research Interests}

\textbf{Representation theory:} double affine Hecke algebras, symplectic
reflection algebras, Cherednik algebras, special functions and orthogonal
polynomials.
\textbf{Algebraic geometry:} Hilbert schemes, Gromov--Witten and
Donaldson--Thomas invariants, affine Springer fibers, noncommutative and
projective geometry.
\textbf{Low-dimensional topology:} knot homology, HOMFLYPT homology,
categorification.
\textbf{Mathematical physics:} integrable systems, inverse scattering,
topological quantum field theory.
