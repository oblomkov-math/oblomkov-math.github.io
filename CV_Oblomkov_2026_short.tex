\section{Research Interests}

Representation theory (double affine Hecke and symplectic reflection
algebras, Cherednik algebras); algebraic geometry (Hilbert schemes,
Gromov--Witten and Donaldson--Thomas invariants, affine Springer fibers);
knot homology and categorification; mathematical physics and integrable
systems.
