\title{Problems on Quadratic forms}
\author{Alexei Oblomkov}
\begin{document}
\maketitle

The quadratic form is a homogeneous function of degree \(2\). That is function on \(\RR^n\):
\[Q(\vec{x})=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}x_jx_j,\quad \vec{x}\in \RR^n.\]

{\bf Theorem} There is a linear change of variables
\[y_i=\sum_{j=1}^nb_{ij}x_j, \quad 1\le i\le n,\]
such that
\[Q(\vec{y})=\sum_{i=1}^n\epsilon_i y_i^2\]
where \(\epsilon_i\) is either \(0\) or \(1\) or \(-1\).
The sequence of \(\epsilon_i\) is called the {\it signature of the quadratic form}.
We also call the change of variables in the theorem {\it simplifying change of variables.} 

For  example if
\[Q(y_1,y_2,y_3,y_4)=y_1^2-y_3^2+y_4^2,\]
then the corresponding quadratic form has signature \((+,0,-,+)\).
The change of variables in the theorem is not unique but the signature of the quadratic form is
unique! That is the number of \(0\), \(+\) and \(-\) does not depend on the change of variables
you choose.

In class we discussed a method for finding a linear change of variables from the theorem.
The  problems below give chance to practice this method and derive some conclusions about
the quadratic forms.


{\bf Problem 1}. Find a simplifying change of variables and the signature for the quadratic form:
\[Q(x_1,x_2)=4x_1^2-4x_1x_2+5x_2^2.\]
The point \((0,0)\) is the critical point of \(Q\), is it maximum, minimum or neither?




{\bf Problem 2}. Find a simplifying change of variables and the signature for the quadratic form:
\[Q(x_1,x_2)=x_1^2+2x_1x_2-20x_2^2.\]
The point \((0,0)\) is the critical point of \(Q\), is it maximum, minimum or neither?




{\bf Problem 4}. Find a simplifying change of variables and the signature for the quadratic form:
\[Q(x_1,x_2,x_3)=2x_1^2-x_1x_2+2x_2^2-x_2x_3+2x_3^2.\]
The point \((0,0,0)\) is the critical point of \(Q\), is it maximum, minimum or neither?




{\bf Problem 5}. Find a simplifying change of variables and the signature for the quadratic form:
\[Q(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)=x_1^2-4x_1x_2+5x_2^2+x_3^2+x_3x_4-16x_4^2.\]
The point \((0,0,0,0)\) is the critical point of \(Q\), is it maximum, minimum or neither?
